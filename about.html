About

The Standard, Not the Slogan.

OnyxAura is a water house, founded in 2026 by a former pilot, for the hotels, cabins and private tables where everything has been considered except, almost always, the water.

Shop the collectionPartner with us

The founder

“I spent my working life in the air, in cabins where every detail had been considered except the water. OnyxAura began there. I wanted a water worthy of India’s finest hotels, flights and training rooms: drawn high in the Himalaya, filtered and sealed with care, in a vessel you are proud to set down. Pick one up and you should feel two things at once: the bottle in your hand, and the quality inside it.”

KunalFounder, OnyxAura · former pilot

What sets it apart

Specific, not adjectival.

Seven things we hold ourselves to, in the order a bottle meets them.

  • 01
    The sourceGlacier- and snow-fed Himalayan springs, between 2,000 and 3,000 metres. The Source
  • 02
    Purification and testingSeven stations, from sand and carbon to ozone and ultraviolet light. Every batch measured; the report on request. The Craft
  • 03
    The vesselGlass for the table, clear cans for everywhere else, and for Gold, fluted glass in a frame plated in 22-carat gold.
  • 04
    Small runsBottled in measured batches, every one coded and recorded.
  • 05
    The Gold editionMade to order, numbered, and delivered by hand. Gold
  • 06
    Delivery and personal serviceAcross India in one to two weeks. Every enquiry answered by a person, within one working day.
  • 07
    Craftsmanship and finishingFilled in a clean room, closed with a tamper band, and checked by our quality team on every run.
