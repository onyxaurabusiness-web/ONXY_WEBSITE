The vessel

Made to outlast what is inside it.

A body of fluted clear glass holds 700 ml. Around it rises a frame of sixteen polished bars plated in 22-carat gold, gathered at a collar under the neck and standing on eight legs laced with a gold lattice; the wide front bar carries the mark and the name. A ring of crystal-cut glass sits beneath a brushed gold cap, engraved with the mountain mark and topped with brushed silver.

The frame is made abroad to our drawing. Every bottle is assembled, checked and numbered in our own workshop near Kempegowda International Airport, Bengaluru, over twenty to thirty days.

From a glacier-fed source.

The ritual

How it is meant to be poured.

Serve it lightly chilled, at 8 to 10 °C, into fine crystal. Unscrew the cap, pour slowly, and let the bottle stand on the table between glasses: it is made to stay in view.

How to receive one

Five quiet steps.

  1. You send a short private note, by the form or to hello@onyxaura.in. No price, no payment, no commitment.

  2. A member of our team replies personally, within one working day.

  3. A private viewing, in person. We bring the bottle to you.

  4. An advance, and your number is set. Twenty to thirty days in the workshop.

  5. Delivered by our own team, with its papers. The balance is settled on delivery.
